Scheduler Failure Evidence
==========================

Configuration:
A pipeline schedule was successfully created and displayed as Active.

UI behaviour:
- A next-run time was initially displayed.
- At the scheduled trigger time, the next-run value disappeared.
- No successful or failed execution appeared in Schedule History.
- No new output was produced in GCS.

Control test:
Running the same pipeline manually succeeded and produced the expected GCS
output.

This behaviour was reproduced using multiple schedule configurations,
including custom recurring schedules.

Rhombus support response:
"You should report this as a bug."

Conclusion:
Scheduling could be configured successfully in the UI, but scheduled
execution did not occur. For this reason, drift experiments were executed
manually and the scheduling limitation is documented explicitly.
